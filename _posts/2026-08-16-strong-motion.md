I picked up a used copy of <cite>Strong Motion</cite> for eight dollars at my local used book store. It had dog-eared pages. Although I buy a substantial number of my books used when I can, dog ears are not something I encounter often.  So it was a shock when I came to my first mutilated page.  My guess is that a former owner was the type to simply use dog-eared book marks as the locations did not seem to mark favourite passages.

I have several authors that I am doing my best to read their works in chronological order, Jonathan Franzen is not one of them.  I started with <cite>Freedom</cite>, which I borrowed from a friend, then moved on to <cite>The Corrections</cite> which is very popular and easy to find a used copy.  So with <cite>Strong Motion</cite> I unintentionally read some of his works in reverse chronological order.

I do enjoy Franzen's characters, they feel very well developed.  In all three of his works that I've read the quality of his characters was pretty much on par.  It's been a while now since I've read his later works but my lasting impression is that the plot in his later novels is a bit more complex.  More interrelation between the characters, and maybe more social commentary through character thoughts.

The longer I read <cite>Strong Motion</cite> the faster I started reading it, until unfortunately, there was no more.  I look forward to picking up some more of his novels.
