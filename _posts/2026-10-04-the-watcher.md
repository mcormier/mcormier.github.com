- The Watcher 1963
- Smog 1965
- The Argentine Ant 1952

I bought <cite>The Watcher</cite> so I could read <cite>The Argentine Ant</cite> as I’m trying to go through Calvino in chronological order.  The easiest way to get it was to buy it with The Watcher. I read these short stories in publish order and not the order they were presented in the book.

<cite>The Argentine Ant</cite> wasn’t a fun romp for me as we recently had a large ant problem at the house, it felt a bit too realistic.  The story itself felt quite dark until the very end when the perspective is shifted.

<cite>The Watcher</cite> was a quite enjoyable read.  I never would have thought that a story about someone working a polling station could work but it was engaging all the way through.  Whereas the Argentine Ant was about a concept, lots of ants everywhere, and living through it, The Watcher creates a character and we see the world through his eyes.

<cite>Smog</cite> was a good balance of descriptions and concept along with well developed characters.  Written two years after <cite>The Watcher</cite>, it shows a steady progression as a writer.  If I had to pick between the two, The Watcher, I'd say, is better but the writing in <cite>Smog</cite> itself is better.  I particularly liked the spin that the head of the anti-pollution magazine ran a polluting factory.

In my youth, I was never a fan of short stories.  I don't know why, maybe they reminded me of English class.  I am starting to appreciate collection of short stories more.  I have some Borges calling my name on my tsundoku.
